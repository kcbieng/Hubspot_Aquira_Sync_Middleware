If that attribute is empty, the mapper infers a team from Aquira sales team, product code, station, sales rep, or advertiser name — in that order. Contacts inherit from their client, then from that client’s contracts.

Default owner is the HubSpot user who owns records for that team when the Aquira sales rep is not in HubSpot. Put that user on the team (main team), then pick them here. Mapped sales reps still win when they exist. HubSpot then fills HubSpot Team from that owner so team-only permissions work.
