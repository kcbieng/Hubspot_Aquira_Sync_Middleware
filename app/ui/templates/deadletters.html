Records whose write failed during a sync. Each open row is retried automatically by the reconciliation job as a fresh targeted sync — never a replay of stored data — so a fix on the Settings or Deal-stages pages takes effect on the very next attempt. Backoff grows with each try; after the retry budget a row shows need review and waits for you. When a later sync writes the record successfully, its row closes itself here. One exception: a failed Aquira client create is held for you immediately — the create can succeed in Aquira even while reporting failure, so only a human (marking the row Resolved) re-arms it, never the machine.
